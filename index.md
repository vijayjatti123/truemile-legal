# TrueMile — Help and support

Automatic mileage logging and tax-ready reports for the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

**Your trips are never uploaded to our servers. Addresses and maps are looked up with Google.**

- [Privacy Policy](privacy/)

## Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: **[connect@vjsoftworks.com](mailto:connect@vjsoftworks.com)**

When reporting a missed or wrong drive, please include your phone model, Android version and the date and time of the drive. In the app, Tracking Health → Share support log creates a file without any locations that helps us investigate.
